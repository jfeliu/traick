Features

Everything you need to stay on top of your projects

💬

Automatic extraction

Claude reads incoming WhatsApp messages and extracts projects, action items, and deadlines without you lifting a finger.

🤖

Replies for you

Traick generates contextual replies in the same language as your contacts, drawing from your full project history.

⏰

Proactive reminders

Scheduled follow-up messages are sent to your contacts automatically when deadlines approach.

🗄️

Self-hosted

All data lives in a local SQLite database on your own machine. No third-party SaaS, no subscription.

How it works

Up and running in minutes

1

Connect your WhatsApp Business number

Create a Meta WhatsApp Cloud API app, set up the webhook pointing to your Traick server, and add your credentials to .env.

2

Start the server

Run uvicorn traick.main:app and expose it publicly with cloudflared or your own VPS. Traick begins listening immediately.

3

Message your number

Send a WhatsApp message to your business number. Traick replies, extracts projects, and schedules reminders — all automatically.
